# N2CIAS visual direction: cinematic, after five films

Replaces the hand-drawn SVG art. Every image on the site should feel like a quiet still from one film about people who feel heard. We borrow each film's **light, colour, framing and mood**. We never use actual frames, posters, characters or actors' likenesses: those are copyrighted, and they would read as borrowed.

## The five references

| Film | What we take | Where on the site |
|---|---|---|
| **Her** (Spike Jonze, 2013) | Warm coral and peach daylight, high-waisted soft clothing, shallow depth of field, a person alone but not lonely, talking to an AI. The core reference: the film is literally about a gentle AI companion. | Home hero, "Apa itu N2CIAS?" |
| **Nanti Kita Cerita Tentang Hari Ini** (Angga Dwimas Sasongko, 2020) | Jakarta homes and streets, warm practical light (lamps, kitchen bulbs), a family slowly learning to talk about feelings. Indonesian, honest, unpolished. | Companies page, story section, "Dari Indonesia, untuk Indonesia" |
| **Perfect Days** (Wim Wenders, 2023) | Calm routine, *komorebi* (sunlight through leaves), long still frames, small rituals: tea, a plant, a walk. What "tenang" looks like. | Toolkit, calming exercises, availability |
| **Before, Now & Then / Nana** (Kamila Andini, 2022) | Painterly Javanese and Sundanese compositions, batik textures, centred symmetrical framing, deep shadow. Heritage with dignity. | Gallery "Dari ELIZA ke N2CIAS", research page |
| **In the Mood for Love** (Wong Kar-wai, 2000) | Night colour: deep teal, rust red, mustard lamplight, rain on glass, figures framed through doorways. Matches our palette exactly. | Night shift (hospitals), dark mode, final call to action |

## Colour and light rules

- **Palette in the image itself:** teal and deep green shadows, rust and terracotta accents, mustard lamp light, cool ice-blue daylight. The site's colours come from these films, so the photos will sit naturally on the page.
- **Light:** always motivated and soft: a window, a desk lamp, dawn, leaves. Never flat office light or clinical white.
- **Grade:** gentle film grain, lifted blacks, slightly warm highlights, as if shot on 35 mm (Kodak Portra / Vision3 look).
- **People:** Indonesian, contemporary, ordinary. Calm faces, often seen in profile or from behind, holding a phone or a cup. Never crying, never in crisis, never a therapist's couch.
- **Framing:** lots of negative space for headlines (keep the left third quiet on wide shots). Frames within frames: doorways, windows, curtains.
- **Avoid:** stock-photo smiles at laptops, brains and glowing circuits, robots, pills, hospital machines, people pointing at screens.

## Prompt template (for AI image tools)

> Cinematic film still, 35 mm, Kodak Portra grade, soft film grain, [SHOT FROM THE LIST], natural motivated light, shallow depth of field, palette of deep teal, rust, mustard lamplight and cool blue, calm and intimate mood, contemporary Indonesia, ordinary Indonesian people, lots of negative space, in the visual spirit of [FILM REFERENCE].

**Negative:** text, logos, watermark, posters, recognisable actors or celebrities, recreated movie scenes, crying, hospital equipment, pills, robots, brains, glowing circuits, stock-photo smiles, oversaturated colours, extra fingers.

## Getting the images

1. **Photograph them.** A half-day shoot in Jakarta with two or three people covers most of the list; this brief is the shoot plan.
2. **Licensed stock photos.** Search Unsplash or Pexels with the shot descriptions; pick frames that obey the colour and light rules.
3. **AI generation.** Use the template above, one consistent seed or style reference across the set.

Send the files and they drop straight into the existing slots: the hero, the four institution cards, the split sections and the gallery entrance.
